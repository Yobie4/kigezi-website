Data Deletion Instructions

How to request removal of your personal information from our systems

Last updated: 12 September 2026

How to Request Deletion

If you would like your personal information removed from the Kigezi Association Management System (KAMS) and our WhatsApp self-service records, please follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to info@kigeziassociation.org, or contact the Secretariat directly at +256 776 455 028, with your full name and member number (if applicable).
  2. State clearly that you are requesting deletion of your personal data.
  3. We will verify your identity as a member (or former member) before processing the request.
  4. We will confirm once your request has been actioned, typically within 14 days.

What Happens to Financial Records

Please note that as a registered Community Based Organisation, Kigezi Association is required to retain core financial transaction records (such as historical savings, loan, and contribution records) for accountability and audit purposes even after a deletion request, in line with our Constitution and applicable record-keeping obligations. Where full deletion is not possible for this reason, we will anonymise personal identifiers wherever practicable and confirm to you what has been retained and why.

WhatsApp Messaging Data

If you have interacted with our WhatsApp self-service, you may also stop future messages at any time by blocking our WhatsApp Business number, in addition to submitting a deletion request as above.
