Privacy Policy

How Kigezi Association collects, uses, and protects your information

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Who We Are

Kigezi Association is a registered Community Based Organisation operating in Kira Municipality, Wakiso District, Uganda (Registration No. WCBO/26/317). We operate the Kigezi Association Management System (KAMS), our internal platform for member records, savings, loans, and communications, and use WhatsApp Business messaging to provide self-service account information to our members.

Information We Collect

As part of managing membership and association finances, we collect and hold: full name, phone number, physical address, and membership/financial records (savings, loan, subscription, fines, and MIFL ledger balances). When you message our WhatsApp Business number, we receive your phone number and the content of your message in order to identify your member account and respond to your request (for example, balance enquiries, loan status, or payment advice slip requests).

How We Use Your Information

We use member information to administer the association's savings, loan, and welfare activities; to generate financial documents such as payment advice slips; to communicate with members about their accounts and association business; and to maintain the accurate financial records required of a registered CBO. WhatsApp messages are used solely to respond to the specific self-service request made and are not used for unrelated marketing.

How We Store and Protect Information

Member records are stored in a password-protected database accessible only to authorised association staff (Finance, Secretariat, and System Administration roles). Our systems are hosted with reputable third-party infrastructure providers (including Render and Cloudflare); messaging is delivered through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, which processes message content under its own privacy terms.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent member information to third parties. Information is shared only: internally among authorised association staff for legitimate association business; with Meta/WhatsApp as necessary to deliver messages you request; and where required by Ugandan law or by the Directorate of Community Based Services as part of our regulatory obligations as a registered CBO.

Data Retention

We retain member records for as long as your membership is active, and thereafter for as long as reasonably necessary to meet our financial record-keeping obligations as a registered association.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information held by the Association at any time. See our Data Deletion page for how to make such a request.
